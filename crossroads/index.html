Plating it at Crossroads

  • Protein first. Get the main from its station before anything else, then build around it.
  • Ask for the portion. At a served station, ask for a double portion of the protein rather than going back for seconds of everything.
  • Half the plate is vegetables. Leafy greens from the salad bar are close to free; take as much as you like.
  • Sauces and dressings on the side. Two tablespoons of ranch is ~140 kcal the plan doesn’t include.
  • Skip the drink calories. Water, sparkling water, black coffee or tea.
  • Desserts aren’t in the plan. If you have one, it comes out of tomorrow’s margin — the weekly trend is what matters.

Weigh-ins

Weigh in the morning, after the bathroom, 3+ times a week. Scale weight swings by a few pounds of water day to day, so the plan reads the 7-day average against the week before.
