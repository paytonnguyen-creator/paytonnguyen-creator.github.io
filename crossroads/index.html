Weigh in
    How to plate it
    • Protein first. Get the main, then build around it.
    • Ounces → hands. A palm-sized piece of cooked meat or fish, about as thick as your palm, is 3–4 oz.
    • Greens are free. Lettuce, spinach and cucumber barely register. Pile them on.
    • Dressing on the side. One ladle of ranch is ~100 kcal that isn’t in the plan. Vinegar or salsa instead.
    • No drink calories. Water, sparkling water, black coffee or tea. Your matcha is already counted.
    • Had dessert? Don’t skip the next meal to make up for it. The weekly weigh-in average is what matters.
